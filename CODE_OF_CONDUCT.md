# Code of conduct

Shortlist is used by people who are looking for work, which is stressful enough. Issues and Discussions here should be a calm place to ask for help and give feedback.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who aren't technical.
- Keep feedback about the app, not the person.
- Respect privacy. Don't post anyone's CV, phone number, email or other personal details, including your own.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, or discrimination of any kind
- Spam, ads, or recruiting posts
- Calling an employer a scam here without evidence. If you think a company is a scam, report it inside the app, where a warning only appears after several users on different networks report it.
- Posting someone's private information

## What happens

I may edit or delete posts, lock threads, or block accounts that break these rules. To report someone's behaviour, use the app's **Feedback** page (it's private), or open an issue if it isn't sensitive.
